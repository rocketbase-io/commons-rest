## Why commons-rest?

We at [rocketbase.io](https://www.rocketbase.io) build many microservices. Tools like
spring-data-rest take flexibility away the moment you leave the happy path, and full-blown
generators bring a mountain of dependencies. `commons-rest` takes a different approach: it
provides the small building blocks every REST service needs — and stays out of your way for
everything else. Your controllers remain plain Spring MVC; you keep full control.
